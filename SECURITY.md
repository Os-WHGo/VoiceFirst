# Security Policy

VoiceFirst is an early prototype, not yet suitable for storing identifiable learner data or recordings.
Do not submit sensitive vulnerabilities or production credentials in public issues. Contact the project maintainers privately using a contact method they explicitly publish later. Until then, do not upload exploit details or personal data to public discussions.
Future releases require explicit mic permissions, secured backend token issuance, minimal retention, and review of dependencies, APIs, and audio storage.
